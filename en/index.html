Screenshot Cleaner

Screenshot Cleaner sorts the screenshots piling up on your phone by app and by type, so you can swipe away the ones you don’t need, right from the list.

FAQ

My screenshots don’t show up

Allow Screenshot Cleaner to access your photos in your phone’s Settings. On iPhone, choose “Allow Full Access” to sort all your screenshots.

A screenshot is in the wrong group

The app guesses the app from the text in each screenshot, so screens with little text may not be sorted correctly. Those go to “Other screens”. Let us know and we’ll use it to improve sorting.

How do I get a deleted screenshot back?

On iPhone, open the Photos app and go to “Recently Deleted”. On Android, check the trash in your gallery app.

How many can I delete for free?

Sorting and browsing are always free. You can delete up to 20 screenshots a month for free. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited deletes. There is no subscription and no ads.

I got a new phone. How do I get my purchase back?

Open the upgrade screen and tap “Restore Purchase”, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Are my screenshots sent anywhere?

No. Reading text and sorting both happen entirely on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
