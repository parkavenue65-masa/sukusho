Privacy Policy

This page explains how Screenshot Cleaner (“the app”) handles your screenshots and data.

1. We don’t collect personal information

The app does not send or collect your name, email address, photos, or any other data. There is no server run by the developer, and the app contains no advertising. (For text-recognition diagnostics on Android, see section 3.)

2. Your screenshots

To sort your screenshots by app and by type, the app reads the screenshots on your phone and recognizes the text in them. Text recognition and sorting happen entirely on your phone, using the text-recognition features built into the system (by Apple on iPhone, and by Google on Android). Your screenshots and the recognized text never leave your phone.

3. Text-recognition diagnostics on Android

On Android, the app uses Google’s ML Kit to recognize text. To improve the service, ML Kit may send Google diagnostic information such as the device model, OS version, app version, a per-install identifier, and processing times. Screenshot images and recognized text are not sent. This information is handled under Google’s privacy policy. Nothing like this is sent on iPhone.

4. Deleting

The app always asks you to confirm before deleting. Deleted screenshots go to “Recently Deleted” in the Photos app on iPhone, or to the trash on Android, and can be restored for a limited time.

5. Data stored on your phone

Recognized text and the screenshots you chose to keep are saved only on your phone, to make sorting faster. Recognized text is not included in iCloud or Google Drive backups. Deleting the app removes this data.

6. Payments

The one-time purchase for unlimited deletes is processed by Apple (the App Store). The developer does not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Whether you have purchased is stored only on your phone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.

8. Contact

Please use the contact address on the support page.
